# What's New in Sovren

---

## v2.0.0 — Production Ready
### March 2026

Sovren 2.0 is here. After months of building, testing, and polishing, every major feature is live and the platform has achieved a 99/100 engineering quality score. Here's everything that's new.

---

### Send Tips Instantly with Lightning Payments

Support your favorite creators with a single tap. Sovren uses the Bitcoin Lightning Network to send payments in seconds with fees so small they're practically free — even for tiny amounts like a few cents. No bank account required, no 2–7 day wait. Your creator gets the money right away, wherever they are in the world.

---

### Subscription Tiers: Become a Regular

Creators can now offer recurring subscription plans at different price levels — think "free follower," "paying supporter," and "inner circle." Subscribe once and your access renews automatically. Creators get reliable monthly income; you get ongoing access to their best work.

---

### Content Shield: Protect Your Work

Original content belongs to its creator. Content Shield automatically generates a fingerprint for everything you publish, so if your writing, images, or videos show up somewhere else without your permission, Sovren can detect it. Your provenance is recorded on the network the moment you publish — no lawyers required.

---

### Creator Circles: Your Private Community

Build a closer community with Creator Circles — private groups for your most dedicated supporters. Share exclusive posts, host discussions, and give your inner circle a space that isn't visible to the public. As a supporter, Circles give you access to content and conversations that don't exist anywhere else.

---

### Mentorship: Learn From the Best

The Mentorship feature connects experienced creators with people who want to grow. Browse the mentor directory, find someone whose work you admire, and book time with them directly on Sovren — no third-party booking tools needed. Experienced creators can list their availability and offer their expertise as a service.

---

### Business Manager: Run Your Creative Business

Stop juggling spreadsheets. Business Manager brings invoices, contracts, and expense tracking into one place. Send professional invoices to clients, keep signed contracts organized, and see your income and expenses at a glance. Built for creators who treat their work like a business, because it is.

---

### Wellness Dashboard: Avoid Burnout Before It Happens

Creating is rewarding — but it can also be exhausting. The Wellness Dashboard tracks your posting patterns, engagement trends, and work habits, then gives you a personalized burnout risk score. When you're heading toward overload, you'll see it before you feel it, with suggestions for how to pace yourself.

---

### Multi-Platform Distribution: Post Everywhere at Once

Write once, publish everywhere. Connect your other social accounts and Sovren will distribute your content to multiple platforms in one step. The unified inbox pulls replies and messages from all your connected platforms into a single feed, so you never miss a conversation no matter where it started.

---

### Discovery: Find Creators You'll Love

The Discovery page makes it easy to find creators by niche, topic, or category. Search and filter to find voices you've never heard of before. For creators, it means new supporters can find you without already knowing your name.

---

### Everything Else

Beyond the headline features, v2.0 includes hundreds of smaller improvements:

- **Dark mode** now works consistently across all pages and components
- **Accessible dialogs** replace browser-native confirm popups throughout the app
- **Real-time observability** — the platform now runs on production-grade monitoring with Prometheus metrics, Sentry error tracking, and health endpoints
- **Fully automated CI/CD** — every change is tested, scanned for security issues, and deployed automatically before it reaches you
- **99/100 quality score** — the highest engineering benchmark we've ever achieved

---

*Sovren is built on the NOSTR protocol and Bitcoin Lightning Network. You own your content, your audience, and your revenue — always.*
